FAQ

The questions people actually ask

Is this therapy? Am I "sick" if I use it?
No on both. Sukoon is wellness counseling for everyday stress — like a physiotherapist for your back, but for a stressed week. No diagnosis, no medication, no labels. If something clinical shows up, your counselor will say so and point you to the right professional.
Why only 20 minutes for the first call?
Because the first call's only job is: make you feel heard, understand your situation, and agree on one next step. 60 minutes on day one is a commitment most stressed people never make. We want the second call, not the perfect first call.
Who hears my problems? Is it private?
One verified counselor. Calls aren't recorded. Your intake answers are used only for your care, deletable on request (DPDP-aligned). Nothing shared with your employer, ever — we have no employer clients in v1.
What does the AI actually do?
Structure, not therapy: a pre-call reflection of what you typed, daily mood/trigger check-ins, and a weekly summary your counselor reads before session two. It never diagnoses, never gives advice, and flags crisis language to a human + helplines immediately.
Hindi or English? Which cities?
Both languages on every call. v1 pilot: Mumbai, Bengaluru, Delhi-NCR, Hyderabad, Pune, Chennai — slots 7 AM to 1 AM IST; most people get one within 2–3 hours. Sara (free AI guide) works 24×7.
What if I cancel or the call doesn't help?
Reschedule free up to 12 hrs before. First-call ₹299: if the call is a no-show from our side, full refund, no questions. Monthly: cancel anytime, effective next cycle.
